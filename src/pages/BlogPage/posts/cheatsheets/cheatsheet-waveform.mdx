The signals a patient monitor records, under the names they carry in its data. What each one measures, and where it
comes from.

## ECG leads

The limb electrodes are RA, LA and LL: right arm, left arm, left leg (RL is the ground). The chest electrodes are V1 to
V6. A lead compares a positive electrode with a negative one, and sees the part of the heart its positive side faces.

### Limb leads (bipolar)

| Lead | + | − | Looks at |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | LA | RA | Lateral wall of the left ventricle |
| II | LL | RA | Inferior wall |
| III | LL | LA | Inferior wall |

### Augmented limb leads (unipolar)

One limb against the average of the other two.

| Lead | + | Looks at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| aVR | RA | Right upper side of the heart, and its cavity |
| aVL | LA | High lateral wall |
| aVF | LL | Inferior wall |

### Precordial (chest) leads

Each chest electrode against the average of RA, LA and LL.

| Lead | Placement | Looks at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| V1 | 4th intercostal space, right of the sternum | Septum, right ventricle |
| V2 | 4th intercostal space, left of the sternum | Septum, anterior wall |
| V3 | Between V2 and V4 | Anterior wall |
| V4 | 5th intercostal space, midclavicular line | Anterior wall, apex |
| V5 | Level with V4, anterior axillary line | Lateral wall |
| V6 | Level with V4, midaxillary line | Lateral wall |

### Which leads look where

| Wall | Leads |
| --- | --- |
| Inferior | II, III, aVF |
| Lateral | I, aVL, V5, V6 |
| Septal | V1, V2 |
| Anterior | V3, V4 |

## Other signals

| Signal | Name | What it shows | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RESP | Respiration | Breathing: chest movement or airflow | Usually the chest's impedance, measured through the ECG electrodes |
| PLETH | Plethysmograph | Blood volume changes with each heartbeat | Pulse oximeter |
| SpO2 | Oxygen saturation | Percent of hemoglobin carrying oxygen | Pulse oximeter |
| ETCO2 | End-tidal CO2 | Exhaled CO2, read at the end of each breath | Capnograph, on the airway tube or a nasal cannula |
| ABP, ART | Arterial blood pressure | Systolic, diastolic and mean, beat by beat | Arterial line (invasive), often in the radial artery |
| NBP, NIBP | Non-invasive blood pressure | Systolic, diastolic and mean, every few minutes | Blood pressure cuff |
| CVP | Central venous pressure | Pressure in the vena cava, next to the right atrium | Central line (invasive) |
| ICP | Intracranial pressure | Pressure inside the skull | A catheter in a ventricle, or a probe in the brain tissue (invasive) |

ABP and ART are the same arterial line: monitors use one label or the other.
