## The short version

Throw an eye, note the angle, move perpendicular to it, throw again. The
intersection is your stronghold. Everyone knows this. The part people get wrong
is which throw matters.

## Why the first angle matters more

Your two throws form a triangle with the stronghold. The precision of the
intersection depends on how close to perpendicular the two sightlines are — a
narrow angle between them means a small error in either direction slides the
intersection a long way.

So the useful move is not throwing the second eye more carefully. It is walking
further sideways before you throw it.

> Roughly 200 blocks perpendicular is enough to make the second throw forgiving.
